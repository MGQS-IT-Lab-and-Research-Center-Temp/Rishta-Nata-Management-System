WHY THIS TASK EXISTS / ROOT CAUSE (deadlock)
===============================================================================
When Bride's Jama'at President clicks Approve on the review dashboard, the form
should advance AwaitingBrideJamaatPresident -> (groom president if different
jama'at else directly) AwaitingRishtanataSecretary. It does NOT: staged deadlock.
The form sits forever at AwaitingBrideJamaatPresident, so groom-president and the
National Rishtanata Secretary never get their turn.

ROOT CAUSE (verified): the flat, status-only president service used by the old
controller Approve only ever wrote Status (and an audit log entry); it NEVER
advanced the fine-grained MarriageFormStage. Advancing the fine-grained stage is
the JOB of the WORKFLOW service (IMarriageFormWorkflowService), which is the
single source of truth (AGENTS.md/architecture). Flat service Apprrove = dead end.
